How I think

Working principles

AI can generate content. It can't decide what's worth saying.

01

Decide what's worth saying first

Most marketing failures are decisions that never got made — dressed up as execution problems.

02

The brief is the deliverable

If we can't write a one-page argument for why the work should exist, the work shouldn't yet exist.

03

Interview the disagreements

The most useful information lives in the gap between what leaders say in public and what they say to each other.

04

Ship it to sales first

If sales can repeat the positioning back in their own words, it's real. If not, it isn't.
