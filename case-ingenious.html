Overview

A publication celebrating Canadian innovation, coordinating contributions from agencies, universities, and government partners — each with their own voice and stake in the story.

Strategy

I contributed content and project-managed the publication, coordinating agencies, universities, government partners, and editorial stakeholders while keeping quality and momentum through production.

Impact

The finished book was co-authored by Governor General David Johnston and Tom Jenkins and became a nationally recognized publication — proof that a single, consistent editorial voice could hold together dozens of outside contributors without losing anyone's material along the way.
